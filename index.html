Why Aio exists

Every complaint dies in the group chat.

Everyone has a problem with something: the prices, the hours, the rules nobody explained. It gets talked about in hallways and group chats, then forgotten.

Not because nobody cares. Because nobody knows how many others care, who should lead, or what the plan is. So the moment passes.

Aio gives that moment a place to grow: a shared problem, a crowd that agrees, a leader they chose, and a plan they can trust.

How it works

From one post to a planned protest.

  1. 1

    Share the problem

    Describe what's wrong at your school, college or workplace. Clear, specific, and in your own words.

  2. 2

    Gather your people

    Members of your community who agree and face the same problem join the protest.

  3. 3

    Vote a leader

    Members vote to appoint a leader. One person, chosen by everyone, keeps the plan together.

  4. 4

    Plan it, safely and easily

    The leader sets each protester's role, secures the resources, sets the goals, and picks the location.

  5. 5

    Meet up, and be heard

    Everyone knows where to be and what to do. Every protest is monitored to keep it peaceful.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Aio?

Aio is a website where people form protests around problems they face at school, college, or work. You share the problem, gather members who agree, vote a leader, and plan the protest together.

What does "Aio" mean?

Aio is a Latin word that means to speak, and to affirm. In other words: I say.

Who can start a protest?

Anyone who faces a real problem in their school, college, or workplace. Members of that community who agree can then join it.

How is the leader chosen?

Members of the protest vote to appoint a leader. The leader is chosen by the people in it, not by whoever posted first.

What does the leader actually plan?

Four things: the roles of each protester, the resources needed, the goals to achieve, and the location where everyone meets up.

Is it safe?

Every protest and event on Aio is strictly monitored. Aio is built for peaceful protests, planned safely and easily.

What happens if someone breaks the rules?

Any protest that violates Aio's policy is removed automatically, and the violation is recorded on each person's profile. For every 3 violations, the police are informed.
